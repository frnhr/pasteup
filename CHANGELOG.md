## 0.2.0

### Added

- Drag to reorder History and Saved clips; order survives restarts.
- Drag outside the picker to delete or unsave a clip.

### Changed

- The clipboard follows the new first clip after reordering or deleting.

## 0.1.0

### Added

- Clipboard history, saved clips, filtering, and keyboard-driven pasting.
